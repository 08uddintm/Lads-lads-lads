The purpose of this app is simple: help the group align on which city works best for everyone based on availability, spend, and overall vibe. Once I review everyone’s responses, I’ll choose the strongest option and build the final itinerary around it.

Step 1: Review the options

Check the four city cards below and look at the weather, cost range, and nightlife angle.

Step 2: Fill in the form

Choose every date window you can do, your budget band, and your top 3 destination ranking.

Step 3: Press Submit by email

This opens an email draft to me with your answers already filled in. You must then press send in your mail app for me to receive it.
